Demo version — SAMPLE data. This standalone demo uses labelled sample schools from Lahore, Sheikhupura and Gujranwala. Entries you add are saved in this browser only (localStorage). A shared database can be connected later with a Supabase Project URL and public anon key. This demo is not connected to the live SIS or IMIS systems; live integration needs the PMIU SIS/IMIS API.
Source: Terms of Reference, November 2026 — Development and Integration of Digital Fund Utilization Tracking Module for Non-Salary Budget (NSB) of Schools, PMIU-PESRP. Go-live target: 12 weeks from contract signing; 12 weeks post-deployment support. Deliverables D-1 to D-11. Payment: 20% (D-1, D-2), 50% (D-3 to D-6), 30% (D-7 to D-11).
